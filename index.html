Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How fast is your delivery within Lagos?

Our same-day delivery service within Lagos typically takes 2-6 hours depending on traffic and distance. For express delivery, we guarantee delivery within 2-4 hours for an additional fee. Interstate deliveries to major cities like Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan are next-day, while other states take 2-3 business days.

Do you deliver to all states in Nigeria?

Yes! We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT. Our network covers major cities with next-day delivery and extends to remote areas within 2-5 business days. We partner with local agents in each state to ensure reliable last-mile delivery.

Is my package insured?

All packages are covered by our basic liability protection. For high-value items, we offer additional insurance up to ₦500,000. Corporate clients enjoy full insurance coverage as part of their contract. Please declare the value of your item when booking.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes, we offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for Lagos deliveries. For interstate deliveries, we require a 50% deposit with the balance paid on delivery. We also accept bank transfers, USSD, and card payments through Paystack.

How do I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment in three ways: (1) Use our online tracking tool on this website, (2) Send your tracking ID via WhatsApp to our support line, or (3) Call our 24/7 helpline. You'll receive SMS updates at every stage of the delivery.

What items do you NOT deliver?

For safety and legal reasons, we do not deliver: illegal substances, weapons, explosives, perishable foods without proper packaging, live animals, and hazardous materials. If you're unsure about an item, please contact us before booking.
